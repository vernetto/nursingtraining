# Appendix A. Examination Checklists by System

Use these checklists as memory aids after learning each examination in the corresponding chapter (Part IV). They are structured as **Preparation → Inspection → Palpation → Percussion → Auscultation → Special tests → Synthesis**. Adapt to the clinical question: a focused examination in an emergency differs from a full systems review. Always: introduce yourself, wash hands, obtain consent, ensure privacy and chaperone as appropriate, expose adequately while maintaining dignity, and examine from the patient's right side.

> **Reminder.** A normal examination does not exclude disease; an abnormal sign is interpreted in context. Document positive and relevant negative findings.

---

## A1. General examination and vital signs

- **Approach**: first impression ("sick or not sick"), age appearance, distress, posture, build, nutrition, hygiene, smell, speech, mood, mobility.
- **Vital signs**: temperature (site), heart rate (rate, rhythm), blood pressure (appropriate cuff; both arms at first visit; lying/standing if postural symptoms), respiratory rate (count for a full 30–60 s while unobserved), SpO₂ (± on room air), pain score, capillary glucose if altered, weight, height, BMI, waist circumference, NEWS2.
- **Hands**: clubbing, leukonychia, koilonychia, splinter haemorrhages, palmar erythema, Dupuytren's, tremor, nail changes, temperature, capillary refill, joint changes, xanthomata.
- **Face**: pallor (conjunctivae), jaundice (sclerae), xanthelasma, cyanosis (central: tongue), parotid swelling, facial asymmetry, ptosis, proptosis, malar rash.
- **Mouth**: hydration, candida, ulcers, dentition, gum hypertrophy, leukoplakia, tonsils.
- **Neck**: lymph nodes (occipital, preauricular, tonsillar, submandibular, submental, anterior and posterior cervical, supraclavicular), thyroid (inspection, swallow, palpation), JVP.
- **Skin**: colour, rashes, bruises, petechiae, lesions, scars, turgor, oedema.
- **Lymph nodes**: axillary, epitrochlear, inguinal; size, consistency, tenderness, mobility, fixity.
- **Hydration**: mucous membranes, skin turgor, JVP, capillary refill, urine output.

---

## A2. Cardiovascular system

1. **Preparation**: patient at 45° (JVP), chest exposed, ECG available.
2. **General**: breathlessness, cyanosis, malar flush, pallor, marfanoid habitus.
3. **Hands/arms**: clubbing, splinters, Osler nodes, Janeway lesions, pulse (rate, rhythm, character at radial; collapsing pulse), radio-radial and radio-femoral delay, BP both arms.
4. **Neck**: carotid pulse (volume, character, bruit), JVP (height above sternal angle <3 cm normal, waveform, hepatojugular reflux, Kussmaul sign).
5. **Precordium inspection**: scars (sternotomy, thoracotomy), pacemaker, visible apex beat, pulsations.
6. **Palpation**: apex beat (position: 5th intercostal space, mid-clavicular line; character: heaving, thrusting, tapping), heaves (parasternal), thrills (aortic, pulmonary, apical).
7. **Auscultation**: S1, S2 (splitting), added sounds (S3, S4, opening snap, ejection click), murmurs (timing, location, radiation, loudness grade 1–6, manoeuvres: expiration for left-sided, inspiration for right-sided, leaning forward for aortic regurgitation, left lateral for mitral stenosis, Valsalva/standing for HCM), carotid and renal bruits, lung bases (crackles).
8. **Peripheral**: sacral and ankle oedema, hepatomegaly/pulsatile liver, ascites; peripheral pulses (femoral, popliteal, posterior tibial, dorsalis pedis), bruits, capillary refill, skin changes, ulcers, ankle-brachial index.
9. **Special**: postural BP, Allen test, cardiac tamponade signs (Beck's triad, pulsus paradoxus), signs of infective endocarditis.
10. **Synthesis**: rhythm, valve lesion, heart failure features, volume status, perfusion.

**Common murmur characteristics**

| Murmur | Timing | Site | Radiation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aortic stenosis | Ejection systolic, crescendo–decrescendo | Right 2nd ICS | Carotids | Slow-rising pulse, narrow pulse pressure, soft S2, thrill |
| Mitral regurgitation | Pansystolic | Apex | Axilla | Displaced apex, soft S1, S3 |
| Mitral stenosis | Mid-diastolic rumble, opening snap | Apex (left lateral) | None | Loud S1, AF, malar flush |
| Aortic regurgitation | Early diastolic | Left sternal edge (sit forward, expiration) | | Collapsing pulse, wide pulse pressure, Austin Flint |
| Tricuspid regurgitation | Pansystolic, louder on inspiration | Left sternal edge | | Prominent v waves, pulsatile liver |
| VSD | Pansystolic | Left lower sternal edge | | Thrill |
| HCM | Ejection systolic, increases with Valsalva | Left sternal edge/apex | | Jerky pulse |
| Innocent flow murmur | Soft ejection systolic | Pulmonary area | | Variation with position, no symptoms |

## A3. Respiratory system

1. **General**: respiratory rate, work of breathing, accessory muscles, ability to speak, cyanosis, cachexia, posture (tripod), chest shape (barrel, pectus), scars, sputum pot.
2. **Hands**: clubbing, tar staining, wasting of small hand muscles (Pancoast), tremor, asterixis (CO₂ retention), pulse.
3. **Face/neck**: central cyanosis, Horner's, JVP, tracheal position, cervical nodes, tracheal tug.
4. **Chest inspection**: symmetry, expansion, scars, radiotherapy marks, deformity.
5. **Palpation**: expansion (upper and lower, 5 cm normal), apex beat, trachea, tactile vocal fremitus, chest wall tenderness, crepitus (subcutaneous emphysema), cricosternal distance.
6. **Percussion**: front, sides, back, comparing sides; resonant, hyper-resonant (pneumothorax, emphysema), dull (consolidation, collapse), stony dull (effusion); diaphragm excursion.
7. **Auscultation**: breath sounds (vesicular, bronchial), added sounds (wheeze, coarse crackles, fine end-inspiratory crackles, pleural rub, stridor), vocal resonance (whispering pectoriloquy).
8. **Other**: peak flow, SpO₂, sputum, temperature, calf swelling (DVT), lymph nodes, abdominal examination for hepatomegaly/ascites.

| Finding | Expansion | Percussion | Breath sounds | Vocal resonance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consolidation | Reduced on side | Dull | Bronchial, coarse crackles | Increased |
| Pleural effusion | Reduced | Stony dull | Reduced/absent (bronchial above) | Reduced |
| Pneumothorax | Reduced | Hyper-resonant | Absent/reduced | Reduced |
| Collapse | Reduced, trachea toward | Dull | Reduced/absent | Reduced |
| Fibrosis | Reduced | Normal/dull | Fine end-inspiratory crackles | Normal |
| COPD/asthma | Reduced bilaterally | Hyper-resonant | Prolonged expiration, wheeze | Normal |

## A4. Abdominal system

1. **Preparation**: supine, one pillow, arms by side, knees slightly flexed, exposed from nipples to knees, warm hands, empty bladder, ask about pain location first.
2. **Hands**: clubbing, leukonychia, palmar erythema, Dupuytren's, asterixis.
3. **Face/mouth/neck**: jaundice, anaemia, spider naevi (chest), parotids, glossitis, ulcers, Virchow's node (left supraclavicular).
4. **Inspection**: contour, scars, stomata, distension (5 Fs: fat, fluid, faeces, flatus, fetus; tumour), visible peristalsis, caput medusae, striae, hernias, bruising (Cullen, Grey Turner), pulsation.
5. **Palpation**: superficial then deep, in all nine regions, away from pain; guarding, rigidity, rebound, masses, organomegaly (liver edge, spleen from the right iliac fossa, kidneys by ballotting), aorta, hernia orifices (cough impulse), Murphy's sign, Rovsing's, psoas and obturator signs.
6. **Percussion**: liver span, shifting dullness, fluid thrill, bladder dullness, spleen (Castell's/Traube's).
7. **Auscultation**: bowel sounds (absent, tinkling), bruits (aortic, renal, hepatic), friction rubs.
8. **Other**: groin, genitalia, hernias, digital rectal examination (when indicated), urinalysis, lower limb oedema, supraclavicular nodes.
9. **Special tests**: McBurney's point, Carnett's sign, Courvoisier's law, Charcot's triad.

| Sign | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Guarding, rigidity, rebound | Peritonitis |
| Murphy's sign | Cholecystitis |
| Rovsing, psoas, obturator | Appendicitis |
| Cullen/Grey Turner | Retroperitoneal haemorrhage, pancreatitis |
| Courvoisier (palpable non-tender gallbladder with jaundice) | Biliary obstruction, often malignant |
| Shifting dullness | Ascites |
| Pulsatile mass with bruit | AAA |
| Hepatomegaly + irregular edge | Metastases |
| Splenomegaly | Haematological, portal hypertension, infection |
| Virchow node | Gastric/abdominal malignancy |

## A5. Neurological system

**Mental status/higher function**: orientation, attention, memory, language (fluency, comprehension, naming, repetition), praxis, neglect; MoCA/MMSE/4AT as needed.

**Cranial nerves**

| Nerve | Test |
|---|---|
| I | Smell (if indicated) |
| II | Acuity, visual fields (confrontation), colour (Ishihara), fundoscopy, pupils (direct, consensual, RAPD) |
| III, IV, VI | Eye movements (H test), ptosis, diplopia, nystagmus, convergence, accommodation |
| V | Facial sensation (three divisions), corneal reflex, jaw strength/jerk |
| VII | Facial expression (UMN vs LMN: forehead sparing), taste |
| VIII | Hearing (whisper, Rinne, Weber), vestibular tests (Dix–Hallpike, head impulse) |
| IX, X | Palate elevation, gag, voice, cough, swallow |
| XI | Shoulder shrug, head turn |
| XII | Tongue protrusion, fasciculations, atrophy |

**Motor**: inspection (wasting, fasciculation, abnormal movements), tone (rigidity, spasticity, clasp-knife, cogwheel), power (MRC grades 0–5; pronator drift), reflexes (biceps C5–6, supinator C6, triceps C7, knee L3–4, ankle S1; plantar response, clonus, Hoffmann), co-ordination (finger–nose, heel–shin, rapid alternating movements, rebound), gait (casual, tandem, Romberg, heel/toe walking).

**Sensory**: light touch, pinprick, temperature, vibration (128 Hz), joint position sense, cortical sensation (stereognosis, graphaesthesia), sensory level, dermatomes.

**Special**: meningism (neck stiffness, Kernig, Brudzinski), straight leg raise, Lasègue, femoral stretch, carotid bruits, speech (dysarthria vs dysphasia), signs of Parkinsonism (tremor, bradykinesia, rigidity, postural instability), UMN vs LMN pattern.

| Feature | UMN | LMN |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Increased (spastic) | Decreased |
| Power | Weakness pyramidal pattern | Focal weakness |
| Reflexes | Increased | Decreased/absent |
| Plantar | Upgoing | Down/absent |
| Wasting | Minimal | Marked |
| Fasciculation | Absent | Present |

## A6. Musculoskeletal system (GALS and joint examination)

**GALS screen** (Gait, Arms, Legs, Spine): ask (pain/stiffness? difficulty dressing? stairs?); inspect standing (front, back, side); gait (normal, heel-toe); spine (lateral flexion, forward flexion 'Schober'); arms (hands behind head, elbows bent, hands out, palms up, fist, pinch, squeeze MCPs); legs (palpate knees for effusion, hip flexion/internal rotation, bulge test).

**Joint examination (LOOK–FEEL–MOVE–FUNCTION)**: swelling, erythema, deformity, muscle wasting, scars; warmth, tenderness, effusion (patellar tap), synovial thickening; active/passive range, crepitus, instability; functional tests (grip, walking, dressing).

| Joint | Key tests |
|---|---|
| Shoulder | Painful arc, Hawkins–Kennedy, Neer, empty can, external rotation lag, apprehension |
| Elbow | Epicondyle tenderness, Cozen's, Mill's |
| Wrist/hand | Phalen's, Tinel's, Finkelstein's, grip |
| Spine | Schober's, SLR, femoral stretch, Fabere |
| Hip | Thomas test, Trendelenburg, internal rotation, FADIR |
| Knee | Effusion (patellar tap, bulge), Lachman, anterior/posterior drawer, valgus/varus stress, McMurray, Thessaly |
| Ankle/foot | Anterior drawer, talar tilt, Thompson test, Achilles palpation, arches |

Look for inflammatory features: morning stiffness >30 min, symmetric small-joint involvement, synovitis (boggy), systemic symptoms; and extra-articular signs (nodules, uveitis, rash, psoriasis, nail pitting).

## A7. Endocrine and thyroid examination

- **General**: weight, build, skin, hair, voice, mental state, fluid balance.
- **Hands**: tremor, sweating, palmar erythema, acropachy, onycholysis, carpal tunnel signs; pulse (AF, rate).
- **Eyes**: exophthalmos, lid retraction, lid lag, ophthalmoplegia, chemosis.
- **Neck**: thyroid swelling (swallow), size, consistency, nodules, bruit, lymph nodes, retrosternal extension (Pemberton's).
- **Reflexes**: ankle jerk relaxation phase (slow in hypothyroidism); proximal myopathy (sit-to-stand).
- **Signs of Cushing's**: central obesity, moon face, striae, bruising, proximal weakness, hypertension.
- **Signs of acromegaly**: coarse features, large hands/feet, prognathism, carpal tunnel.
- **Diabetes foot**: skin, ulcers, deformity, pulses, monofilament, vibration, ankle reflex.
- **Diabetic eye**: fundoscopy, visual acuity.
- **Pituitary**: visual fields (bitemporal hemianopia).

---

## A8. Breast, genital and pelvic examination (when indicated)

- **Breast**: chaperone; inspection (symmetry, skin, nipples), palpation (all quadrants, tail, axilla), lymph nodes; document lumps by clock position and distance from the nipple.
- **Male genitalia**: inspection (penis, foreskin, urethral meatus), scrotum (palpate each testis, epididymis, cord), transillumination, cough impulse, hernias, prostate (DRE: size, consistency, nodules, tenderness).
- **Female pelvic**: chaperone; inspection, speculum (cervix, discharge, bleeding), bimanual (uterine size, position, adnexal masses, cervical excitation tenderness), swabs (NAAT), cervical screening.
- **Rectal**: indications and consent; sphincter tone, masses, blood, stool colour, prostate.
- **Pregnancy**: fundal height, lie, presentation, fetal heart, BP, oedema, urinalysis (protein), reflexes.

---

## A9. Head, ears, nose, throat and eyes

- **Eyes**: acuity (Snellen), fields, pupils (size, reactivity, RAPD), extraocular movements, red reflex, fundoscopy (disc margins, cup:disc ratio, vessels, haemorrhages, exudates, papilloedema), lids, conjunctiva, cornea (fluorescein), intraocular pressure (if trained), swinging flashlight test.
- **Ears**: pinna, mastoid tenderness, otoscopy (canal, tympanic membrane colour, light reflex, perforation, effusion), Rinne, Weber, hearing (whisper test).
- **Nose**: patency, septum, turbinates, polyps, bleeding, sinus tenderness.
- **Mouth/throat**: lips, buccal mucosa, tongue (including underside), gums, teeth, tonsils (size, exudate), uvula, posterior pharynx, palate; neck nodes.
- **Cranial**: scalp tenderness, temporal arteries (pulsation, tenderness), skull deformity, bruits.

---

## A10. Skin, hair and nails

- **Describe lesions**: distribution, morphology (macule, papule, plaque, nodule, vesicle, bulla, pustule, wheal, erosion, ulcer, scale, crust, lichenification, atrophy), colour, border, size, surface, arrangement (linear, grouped, annular), symmetry.
- **ABCDE of melanoma**: Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Colour variation, Diameter >6 mm, Evolving; look at the "ugly duckling".
- **Dermoscopy** if trained.
- **Palpation**: warmth, tenderness, blanching (diascopy), induration, fluctuance, Nikolsky sign.
- **Systemic clues**: mucosal involvement, lymph nodes, hepatosplenomegaly, joints.

---

## A11. Paediatric examination (age-adapted)

- **Observation first** from a distance: alertness, interaction, colour, work of breathing, cry, activity.
- **Vital signs** by age (see Appendix C); weight, length/height, head circumference; growth centiles.
- **Hydration**: fontanelle, mucous membranes, capillary refill, skin turgor, tears, urine output.
- **ENT last** (otoscopy, throat), **heart/chest early** while calm; abdominal palpation with distraction; hips (Barlow, Ortolani in neonates), spine, genitalia, red reflex, primitive reflexes, development (milestones, Denver-style).
- **Red flags**: grunting, nasal flaring, retractions, cyanosis, bulging fontanelle, neck stiffness, non-blanching rash, abnormal tone, bilious vomiting.

---

## A12. Mental state examination

- **Appearance and behaviour**: grooming, dress, eye contact, psychomotor activity, rapport, abnormal movements.
- **Speech**: rate, volume, tone, flow, coherence.
- **Mood and affect**: subjective mood, objective affect (range, congruence, reactivity).
- **Thought**: form (flight of ideas, loosening, tangentiality), content (delusions, obsessions, preoccupations, suicidal and homicidal ideation).
- **Perception**: hallucinations (auditory, visual, tactile), illusions, depersonalisation.
- **Cognition**: orientation, attention, memory, executive function.
- **Insight and judgment**.
- **Risk assessment**: self-harm, suicide (plan, intent, means), harm to others, vulnerability, protective factors.

---

## A13. Emergency "primary survey" checklist (ABCDE)

| Step | Look/Listen/Feel | Act |
|---|---|---|
| **A**irway | Talking? Stridor, snoring, gurgling, obstruction | Airway manoeuvres, suction, adjuncts, call for help |
| **B**reathing | RR, SpO₂, work of breathing, chest movement, percussion, auscultation, trachea | Oxygen (target 94–98%; 88–92% in hypercapnic risk), bronchodilators, decompression, bag-valve-mask |
| **C**irculation | HR, BP, capillary refill, JVP, skin, urine output, haemorrhage, ECG, lactate | IV/IO access, bloods, fluids/blood, vasopressors, stop bleeding |
| **D**isability | AVPU/GCS, pupils, glucose, limb movement, seizures | Treat hypoglycaemia, seizures, naloxone |
| **E**xposure | Full exposure, temperature, rash, injuries, back | Warm/cool, log-roll, analgesia, document |

Reassess after each intervention; call for senior help early.

---

## A14. Completing the examination: documentation template

> **Patient** (ID, age, sex) — **Date/time** — **Examiner**
> **General**: appears [well/unwell]; vitals (T, HR, BP, RR, SpO₂, glucose); weight/BMI.
> **System findings**: [CVS; RS; GI; CNS; MSK; skin; others] — document positive findings and pertinent negatives.
> **Impression**: working diagnosis and differential with probabilities.
> **Plan**: investigations, treatment, escalation, safety-net, follow-up, who is responsible.
> **Discussion with patient/family**: key points, understanding, consent.

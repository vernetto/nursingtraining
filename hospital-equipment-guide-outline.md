# Hospital Equipment: A Complete Guide to Devices, Machines, Tools and Consumables

## Part I: Foundations
1. **How a hospital is organized.** Departments, flows of patients, staff and supplies, and where equipment lives.
2. **Classification of medical devices.** Risk classes, CE/FDA/WHO frameworks, reusable vs single-use, capital equipment vs consumables.
3. **Safety principles.** Electrical safety, gas safety, alarms, human factors, and incident reporting.
4. **Lifecycle of equipment.** Procurement, installation, calibration, maintenance, decommissioning, and biomedical engineering.

## Part II: The Bedside Basics
5. **Hospital beds and patient handling.** Electric and ICU beds, mattresses, pressure-relief surfaces, stretchers, wheelchairs, lifts, and transfer boards.
6. **Vital sign monitoring.** Thermometers, sphygmomanometers, pulse oximeters, ECG, and capnography.
7. **Patient monitors.** Multiparameter monitors, central stations, telemetry, and alarm management.
8. **Basic examination tools.** Stethoscope, otoscope, ophthalmoscope, reflex hammer, tuning fork, penlight, and tape measure.

## Part III: Medication and Fluid Delivery
9. **Vascular access.** Peripheral cannulas, central lines, PICCs, ports, arterial lines, and intraosseous devices.
10. **Infusion devices.** Gravity sets, volumetric pumps, syringe pumps, PCA pumps, and enteral feeding pumps.
11. **Syringes, needles and injection devices.** Types, gauges, safety needles, pens, and autoinjectors.
12. **Fluids, blood and blood products.** Crystalloids, colloids, transfusion sets, blood warmers, and cell savers.
13. **Medication management.** Dispensing cabinets, crash carts, labeling, barcode systems, and cold storage.

## Part IV: Respiratory and Airway
14. **Oxygen systems.** Wall outlets, cylinders, concentrators, masks, nasal cannulas, and high-flow devices.
15. **Airway management.** Airways, laryngoscopes, endotracheal tubes, supraglottic devices, tracheostomy tubes, and cricothyrotomy kits.
16. **Ventilation.** Bag-valve-mask, transport ventilators, ICU ventilators, CPAP/BiPAP, and ECMO.
17. **Suction, nebulizers and chest drainage.** Suction units, spacers, chest tubes, and drainage systems.
18. **Respiratory diagnostics.** Spirometers, peak flow meters, and blood gas analyzers.

## Part V: Cardiovascular and Emergency
19. **ECG and cardiac diagnostics.** 12-lead ECG, Holter, stress testing, and echocardiography.
20. **Defibrillators and pacing.** Manual, AED, transcutaneous pacing, and implantable devices.
21. **Resuscitation equipment.** Crash cart contents, CPR aids, and mechanical compression devices.
22. **Cardiac catheterization and interventional tools.** Catheters, guidewires, stents, balloons, and hemodynamic monitoring.
23. **Cardiac surgery and support.** Heart-lung machine, IABP, and ventricular assist devices.

## Part VI: Diagnostic Imaging
24. **X-ray and fluoroscopy.** Fixed and portable units, radiation protection, and lead aprons.
25. **CT scanners.**
26. **MRI.** Safety zones, contraindications, and coils.
27. **Ultrasound.** Probes, point-of-care devices, and uses by specialty.
28. **Nuclear medicine and PET.**
29. **Mammography, DEXA and specialized imaging.**
30. **PACS and imaging IT.**

## Part VII: Laboratory and Point-of-Care Testing

32. **Hematology and coagulation analyzers.**
33. **Clinical chemistry and immunoassay analyzers.**
34. **Microbiology.** Incubators, culture systems, mass spectrometry, and molecular platforms.
35. **Point-of-care tests.** Glucometers, rapid tests, blood gas, and troponin devices.
36. **Pathology and blood bank.** Microtomes, staining, and cross-matching equipment.

## Part VIII: Surgery and Anesthesia
37. **Operating theatre layout and fixed equipment.** Tables, surgical lights, pendants, and laminar flow.
38. **Anesthesia machines and monitoring.** Vaporizers, breathing circuits, depth-of-anesthesia monitors, and warming devices.
39. **Surgical instruments.** Scalpels, forceps, scissors, retractors, clamps, needle holders, and instrument sets by specialty.
40. **Energy devices.** Electrosurgery, ultrasonic and vessel-sealing devices, lasers, and argon plasma.
41. **Endoscopic and minimally invasive surgery.** Laparoscopy towers, cameras, insufflators, and robotic systems.

44. **Surgical drapes, gowns, gloves and counting systems.**

# Suggested Question Record Format

Each question should contain:

- `id`
- `category`
- `subcategory`
- `topic`
- `difficulty`
- `question_type`
- `question`
- `options`
- `correct_answer`
- `explanation`
- `learning_objective`
- `anatomical_structures`
- `clinical_relevance`
- `image_required`
- `source_reference`

Example:

```yaml
id: ANAT-00001
category: Skeletal System
subcategory: Skull
topic: Temporal Bone
difficulty: Basic
question_type: multiple_choice

question: >
  Which structure is located on the temporal bone?

options:
  A: Foramen magnum
  B: Mastoid process
  C: Crista galli
  D: Mental foramen

correct_answer: B

explanation: >
  The mastoid process is a prominent projection of the temporal bone
  located posterior to the external acoustic meatus.

learning_objective: >
  Identify major anatomical landmarks of the temporal bone.

anatomical_structures:
  - temporal bone
  - mastoid process

clinical_relevance: >
  The mastoid region is clinically important in mastoiditis and
  surgical approaches to the temporal bone.

image_required: false
